WHAT'S INSIDE?

  • You're Ball – a small yellow ball living in a tiny green world with 6 flowers. Move with arrows or WASD, open chests, collect scores. But beware: chests can trap you and take half your score! 😱
  • First Raylib project – simple, chaotic, and built with love. C++ / CMake / Raylib. Graphics, sounds and textures from free assets on itch.io.
  • Unofficial sources warning – only download from this repo or itch.io. Other sources are unofficial forks/minor. Stay safe!

HOW TO RUN

LINUX – .tar.gz
bash ./run.sh
MACOS – .zip
bash ./run-macos.sh
WINDOWS – .exe
double-click to run. Unsigned build – SmartScreen may warn.
